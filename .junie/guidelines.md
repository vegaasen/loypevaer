# Junie Guidelines

## CRITICAL: Immediate Response Protocol (Mandatory)

If a prompt contains a direct question or ambiguous requirements, do NOT start or continue any file modifications, searches, or terminal commands. Your very first action MUST be to ask clarifying questions and gather enough information to fully understand the intent. Respond with only your questions — do not perform any other actions. Do not resume any coding task until all ambiguities are resolved and you have a clear understanding of what is expected.
